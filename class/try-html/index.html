0 · Read me first

HTML is the language webpages are written in. It works with tags — instructions in angle brackets that tell the browser what each piece of content IS. HTML funciona con etiquetas: instrucciones entre < > que dicen qué es cada cosa.

Most tags come in pairs: <h1> opens, </h1> closes. The / means “end of this.”

The editor already has code in it. Change the name to YOUR name. Watch the right side change as you type — that’s the browser re-reading your HTML.

Try it: change the <h1> to say something only you would write. Add a second <p> paragraph about your favorite game.

✓ Self-check: two paragraphs on screen, and your own words in the big heading.

2 · Headings (5 min)

Headings go <h1> (biggest) down to <h6> (smallest). A page usually has ONE h1, then h2s for sections — like chapters. Los encabezados organizan la página como capítulos.

<h2>About me</h2>
<h2>My favorite games</h2>
Try it: add two <h2> section headings below your intro, each with a <p> under it.

✓ Self-check: the h2s are smaller than the h1, bigger than the paragraphs.

3 · Lists (10 min)

Two kinds: <ul> = bullet list (unordered), <ol> = numbered list (ordered). Each item lives in <li>. ul = lista con viñetas; ol = lista numerada; li = cada elemento.

<ul>
  <li>First thing</li>
  <li>Second thing</li>
</ul>
Try it: under one h2, add a <ul> of 3 things you’re good at. Under the other, add an <ol> of the 3 steps of your morning, in order.

✓ Self-check: bullets on one list, numbers on the other. Numbers appear without you typing them — the browser counts.

4 · Bold, italics, lines (5 min)

<strong> = bold (important) · <em> = italics (emphasis) · <hr> = a horizontal line, no closing tag needed.

<p>This is <strong>serious</strong> and this is <em>stylish</em>.</p>
<hr>
Try it: bold ONE phrase, italicize ONE word, and put an <hr> between your sections.
5 · Links (5 min)

The <a> tag makes a link. The address goes in href:

<a href="https://canutillocs.com/class/game-bid-pbl/">our project hub</a>
Try it: add that link to your page. Click it in the preview — then come back.

✓ Self-check: the link is blue and underlined, and only the words between the tags are clickable.

7 · THE BUILD: “About My Game” (20–25 min)

Now build a real page about the game your crew studied (or a game you know well). Use your Field Survey brain: what does a player do? how do you win? Construye una página sobre el juego de tu equipo.

Requirements — your page needs all 7:

  1. One <h1> — the game’s name
  2. Two or more <h2> sections (like “How it works”, “Why it’s fun”)
  3. Three or more <p> paragraphs
  4. A <ul> with at least 3 rules of the game
  5. An <ol>: what a player does on one turn, in order
  6. At least one <strong> AND one <em>
  7. An <hr> between sections

Stretch (if time): a paragraph — should this game be a phone app? What survives the jump, what gets lost? You already know this from the survey. And try <h1 style="color:darkblue"> for color.
