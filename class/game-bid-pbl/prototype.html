Your wireframes already passed the audit. That means the hard thinking is done and the paper is now your instructions. Today you type it up, make it clickable, and hand it to another crew to break. Tus esquemas ya pasaron la auditoría: el papel es ahora tu instructivo.

Step 1 — One slide per screen

Open your crew’s copy of the starter file (in Google Classroom). The slides are tall because a phone is tall — that is your portrait constraint, already handled.

  1. Put each screen on its own slide — the same boxes, the same labels, the same one job written on the slide.
  2. Transcribe. Do not redesign. Reopening the design now costs you the build, and the version you are about to throw away is the one that passed the fence audit.
  3. Three to five screens — the fence has not moved. Delete the starter slides you do not need.
  4. Put them in walk order: Start, Play, Win. This matters more than you think — see the warning below.

Una diapositiva por pantalla; copia tu esquema tal cual; de 3 a 5 pantallas, en orden de recorrido.

Step 2 — Make it look deliberate

This is criterion 3, and it is the easiest one to lose. Deliberate does not mean plain — it means every choice on the screen is one you could defend out loud.

  1. ONE small palette. The same few colors on every screen. Nine colors reads as nine accidents.
  2. One or two fonts. Not five.
  3. Buttons that look like buttons — and look the same on every screen, so a player learns them once.

The test: finish the sentence “we chose this because…” for anything on your screen. If you cannot, it is decoration. Pocos colores, una o dos fuentes, botones que parezcan botones — y que puedas justificar cada decisión.

Step 3 — The five clicks (this is the whole trick)

Every tappable box on your wireframe becomes a link to another slide. Do it for every tap, on every screen.

  1. Click the box you want to be tappable. Haz clic en la caja que se toca.
  2. Press Ctrl + K  (or Insert → Link).
  3. Click “Slides in this presentation.” Elige “Diapositivas de esta presentación”.
  4. Pick the screen it should open → Apply.
  5. Click Present and try it. Links only work in Present mode. Los enlaces solo funcionan en modo presentación.
Know this before your walk. In Present mode, a click on empty space jumps to the next slide. Nothing is broken — that is just how Slides works. Two consequences, and they are the difference between a prototype that survives Bid Day and one that does not:
  1. The client clicks only your labeled buttons. Anything you did not label, he will not touch.
  2. Your slides must sit in walk order, so a stray click still lands somewhere sensible instead of in the middle of your app.
Un clic en un espacio vacío avanza a la siguiente diapositiva: etiqueta todo lo que se toca y ordena tus pantallas.

Step 4 — Test it, then let another crew break it

First, you. Press Present and walk your own app Start → Play → Win. Every button.

Then, them. Trade with another crew. The reading crew walks your app clicking only your labeled buttons, and your crew stays completely silent — no hints, no “you have to click the…”. They write down every dead end and every wrong landing.

If they need you to talk, the prototype failed, not the reader. On Bid Day the client drives and your crew is silent — this is that, with a friendlier judge. Otro equipo lo recorre en silencio; si necesitan tu ayuda, falló el prototipo, no el lector.

When it breaks — and it will

Finding a broken link today is a good day. The alternative is the client finding it on Bid Day. That is what debugging is: you look for what is wrong on purpose, before it matters.

What you seeWhat it usually is
Nothing happens when I clickYou are not in Present mode — or you linked the TEXT and clicked the BOX. Link the box itself.
It goes to the wrong screenClick the box, click the link, Remove, and redo steps 2–4.
Dead end — no way outEvery screen needs at least one link off it. There is no back button on a prototype the client is holding.
A click jumps somewhere randomThat was empty space, not a button. Label it, or accept it and fix your slide order.
