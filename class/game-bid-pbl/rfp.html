Read this box first: criteria vs. constraints

CRITERIA · criterios
Judged — degrees of good. The scale we score your bid on. You can do well or poorly on a criterion.
CONSTRAINTS · restricciones
Hard fences — in or out. A bid that breaks a constraint isn’t a lower score. It’s OUT.

The fence is not your enemy. A project with no rules produces mush; the fence is what makes your bid creative.

CRITERIA — what a winning bid maximizes (this becomes the bid rubric)

  1. The core loop survives. (core loop · ciclo central — the small set of actions a player repeats every turn; the part that IS the game) Someone who loves the original recognizes it within ten seconds.
  2. The screens work. Start → Play → Win/End, walkable in the prototype without your crew explaining it.
  3. It looks deliberate. Readable text, consistent colors and fonts, buttons that look tappable.
  4. The pitch names the trade. The ONE thing the phone made better, and the ONE thing you protected from being lost.
  5. Professional correspondence throughout. Every email in the standing five-marks format. The client notices.

CONSTRAINTS — the fence (in or out, no negotiation)

  1. One game from the class library (a card game your crew actually played counts).
  2. Prototype in Google Slides only — linked and clickable. No code, no app builders. (Code comes after the contract is awarded.)
  3. At least 3 screens, at most 5.
  4. Phone-shaped: portrait, playable with one hand.
  5. The classic stays recognizable: speed it up, streamline it — never turn it into a different game.
  6. Everything original: no copied art, logos, or screenshots. Draw your own.
  7. Audience is FAMILIES: a 9-year-old can play it, a grandparent can read it.
  8. Money is out of scope: the app is assumed free to download — no ads or prices in the bid.

DELIVERABLES — what your crew turns in on Bid Day

  1. Wireframes — your paper screen sketches (built next week).
  2. A clickable prototype in Google Slides — every screen reachable by tapping.
  3. A 90-second pitch, delivered live while the client walks your prototype.

Entregables: bocetos en papel, prototipo navegable en Slides, y una presentación de 90 segundos.
