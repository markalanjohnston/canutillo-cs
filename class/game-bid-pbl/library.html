The five library norms — callable, like all norms

  1. One crew, one box — your names are on the checkout Form.
  2. Pieces live on the board or in the bag. Never in pockets.
  3. Count at checkout, count at return — the slip in the bag is the truth.
  4. The box goes home assembled to its shelf number: bag sealed, lid on.
  5. Missing piece? Report it THAT MINUTE. No blame — counting is the norm working.

First unboxing — the ritual

  1. Open carefully. Punch cardboard slowly — no tearing.
  2. Find the contents list on the box. Copy it onto the piece slip.
  3. Count every piece against it. All there? Sign the slip.
  4. Pieces into the zip bag. The slip lives in the bag forever.

Checking out (and back in)

The Checkout Form is pinned in Google Classroom: OUT or IN · period · crew number · shelf number · pieces counted? One entry when you take a game, one when you bring it back. That trail is the library’s memory.
