Where this started 3½ min

Board games have always been built with the technology of their era — hand-painted boards, then cheap mass printing, then games that mirrored their moment (a moon-landing game right after Apollo). Cardboard was a technology. So is the phone in your pocket. That jump is the whole project.

Right now

  1. Field Survey — every player submits their OWN, in Google Classroom. It’s your research timesheet: what a turn looks like, how you win, luck vs. choices, and the client question — one thing a phone makes BETTER, one thing that gets LOST.
  2. Know / Need-to-Know sheet — parked in your crew folder. Over the weekend, think like a bidder. Don’t solve it; sharpen it.
  3. Your one question — when the RFP opens Sept 10, the client answers exactly ONE emailed question per crew. Format matters: see Email like a pro.
