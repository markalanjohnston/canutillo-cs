Register is real. The same ask, two ways: “yo can u fix my grade” gets deleted; “Could you tell me what I missed on Tuesday?” gets answered within the hour. Bosses, coaches, colleges, clients — email is the front door, and the reader decides in five seconds. El registro importa: el mismo mensaje, escrito profesionalmente, SÍ recibe respuesta.

The five marks — scored 0–5, one point each

Every real email you send Mr. Johnston gets a reply with a single number. Count your own marks before you hit send.
1 · Subject · asunto
Starts FoCS P# (your period), then YOUR words saying what it’s about. The subject is how busy people sort — blank or vague means unread.
2 · Body · cuerpo
Greeting + who you are + ONE clear ask. One email, one question.
3 · Signature · firma
A closing (“Thank you,”) and your full name. Every time.
4 · Grammar · gramática
Capitals, punctuation, complete sentences. Not texting.
5 · Formatting · formato
Line breaks between greeting, body, and signature. No wall of text.

To · CC · BCC · Reply All

To · para
The person you expect ACTION from.
CC · con copia
Kept in the loop — reads it, does nothing.
BCC · copia oculta
A privacy tool: hides a big list of addresses from each other. Not sneaky — considerate.
Reply vs. Reply All · responder a todos
Answer the SENDER unless every single recipient truly needs your answer. Reply-All storms are how adults lose an afternoon.
Stuck? Start from the frame:
Good morning Mr. Johnston,
My name is ______ and I am in your Period ___ class. ______ (one clear ask — could you tell us… / could you check…)
Thank you,
______
For the project: the client answers questions by email only, one per crew, in exactly this format — a malformed email gets one reply: “Resend professionally and I’ll answer.” The five marks aren’t a school thing. They’re the job.
